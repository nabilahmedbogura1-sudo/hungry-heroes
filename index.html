THAN A BURGER.

Hungry Heroes is a superhero-themed restaurant in Rajshahi built around burgers, snacks, power bowls and a lively casual-dining experience. The restaurant is located on the fifth floor of Sohidullah Tower 02 in Ranibazar.

The current online menu highlights Heroes Signature Burgers, Headquarters Burgers, Super Stacks, Snacks and Power Bowls — with delivery and dine-in options listed publicly.
